\section{What the first part establishes}\label{sec:bilan}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The judge is a maximum, and on this machine the disk holds it.} For gpt-oss-120b in the retained locality scenario, the disk takes 157\,ms per token, the link 58, the GPU 36 (\cref{sec:exemple-pose}). Any lever that does not touch $h_s B_e/(r_d W_{\mathrm{D}})$ is, here, ornament (\cref{cor:ornement}).
\item \textbf{The disk delivers 47\,\% of its rating}, because the volume is encrypted by BitLocker and goes through Intel RST VMD (\cref{ch:machine}): the largest lever of the machine is not software.
\item \textbf{Every bit removed is worth as much as every byte of throughput gained} (elasticities $\pm 0.98$, \cref{prop:elasticites}); extreme quantization and lossless compression act on the same term, and the Shannon bound says what is left to take (\cref{ch:quant}).
\item \textbf{The cache hit rate decides everything for a mixture of experts} (\cref{ch:chargement}): between uniform routing and a fitted LRU, the ceiling of A changes by a factor of 3.
\item \textbf{Speculation cannot be judged without expert reuse}: the condition $\alpha > 1 - \rho$ (\cref{prop:spec-rho}) replaces the ``never'' of \cref{ch:sparsite}; on gpt-oss-20b, $\rho = 0.577$ (\cref{sec:premieres-mesures}).
\item \textbf{Memory can stop depending on the model}: A0 serves a 63.4\,GB model with 1.9\,GB of peak RAM, and the minimal window goes down to 0.64\,GB (\cref{sec:fenetre-chiffree}).
\item \textbf{A new mechanism exists, narrower than announced}: the exact refinement of a native 4-bit code costs nothing in entropy (\cref{prop:raffinement}) and its base loses 0.27 bit less than truncation at equal error; but once the complements are counted, its throughput gain is only real at the price of a tunable share of precision (\cref{sec:correction-b1}).
\end{enumerate}

\section{What is solid, what is fragile}\label{sec:solide-fragile}

\paragraph{Solid.} The stage throughputs, each measured with a witness that can fall, in ABBA series, and redone by a second path (system counter for the disk, encryption API for BitLocker); the theorems and their proofs (\cref{thm:toit,thm:amortissement}, \cref{prop:raffinement,prop:elasticites}); the exactness of the rANS decoder on the 108 tensors of gpt-oss-120b; the inventory of models read from their headers, redone on the local file.

\paragraph{Fragile.} Everything that depends on locality: the ceilings of A and B rest on a power law fitted at one point (\cref{hyp:beta}) whose shape is already contradicted on gpt-oss-20b; the llama.cpp baseline of gpt-oss-120b, still estimated; the quality of B, only estimated by a linearity valid for small errors; several neighbouring works, read only in abstract.

\section{The limits of the method}\label{sec:limites-methode}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{One machine.} Everything is measured on Stone's laptop; it is the target, but the conclusions about the limiting stage change with another disk (an unencrypted volume hands the lead to the link, \cref{ch:futurs}).
\item \textbf{A shared machine.} Other agents were measuring at the same time; ``best'' throughputs are lower bounds of the idle machine, and each pass logs the foreign load.
\item \textbf{Two machines in one.} On battery, the GPU, the link and the CPU change regime (\cref{sec:batterie}); all retained measurements are on mains power, and the power state is logged with each pass (\cref{ann:protocoles}).
\item \textbf{A quality judge calibrated on another model.} The budget $\kappa = 0.0055$ comes from Qwen3.5-4B; it must be remeasured on gpt-oss.
\item \textbf{Literature in abstract.} Several neighbours of B (TRACE, MatGPTQ, AnyBCQ) are read only in abstract: nothing is published before they are read in full.
\item \textbf{A tooling choice that closes a door.} J1 will be in CUDA (\cref{sec:decision}): the program will require an NVIDIA GPU until the Vulkan port.
\end{itemize}

\section{Reading ``without RAM or anything''}\label{sec:lecture-discussion}

The retained reading (\cref{def:fenetre}) was never substituted for Stone's request: it is his to confirm. The numbers make it concrete. Zero RAM is impossible; a RAM that does not depend on the model is reachable, and its price can be read on \cref{fig:plafond-ram}: each GB of RAM given back to the system costs about 0.24 token/s in the fitted-LRU scenario. ``Without RAM'' becomes a knob, which Stone can turn according to what else he does on his machine.

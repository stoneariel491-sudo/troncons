\section{The deciding measurements, first}\label{sec:mesures-decident}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The curve $h(c)$ and the reuse $\rho$ of gpt-oss-120b}, on its own routing traces. They replace \cref{hyp:beta} in every ceiling; the 20b measurement (\cref{fig:localite-mesuree}) already says the assumed shape is wrong. Witness: the replica of llama.cpp's cache replayed on the traces, which must give back the numbers of its log.
\item \textbf{The llama.cpp baseline of gpt-oss-120b in ABBA series}, on mains power, following the protocol of \cref{sec:protocole-jugement}: this is the first measurement that can kill A (the $\times 2.5$ to $\times 17$ ratio of study 07 rests on an estimated baseline).
\item \textbf{The Q0 bench}: the quality of B without an engine, by static MXFP4 files (base snapped onto existing E2M1 values) judged by llama.cpp, and the frontier of uniform requantizations. This is the measurement that decides J2.
\item \textbf{The gap between two correct engines on gpt-oss}, which recalibrates the quality budget $\kappa$ of \cref{def:probleme}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Milestone J1: building A}\label{sec:construire-j1}

The arbiter splits the work into five work packages with disjoint files, each closed by a witness, its break and a declared threshold (\fichier{decisions/ARCHITECTURE.md}, § 6):
\begin{description}
\item[F, format and coder]: rewriting the original GGUF into a \texttt{.trc} v1.0 file; witness: every decoded chunk equals the original, byte for byte.
\item[E, byte path]: IOCP reader at expert size into pinned memory, DMA; witness: end-to-end throughput exceeds no stage, and bytes arrive exact.
\item[K, kernels]: per-lane-table rANS decoder, MXFP4 product, gpt-oss forward pass; witness: the already measured WGSL kernels, on the same bytes (second path).
\item[R, reference and judge]: a double-precision CPU reference, the quality judge, the Q0 bench.
\item[O, cache, scheduler, engine]: expert cache in VRAM, miss queue, modes A0 and A; witness: bit-identical logits between the coded path and the raw path.
\end{description}
The milestone is judged on gpt-oss-120b: tokens/s, peak RAM and VRAM, bytes read according to the system counter, and physical efficiency, i.e.\ measured throughput over the ceiling of \cref{eq:jeton} recomputed with the hit rate counted by the engine. Then J1+ adds one-layer-ahead prefetching and an SLRU cache, and is judged against the best measured llama.cpp baseline.

\section{The gesture that falls to Stone}\label{sec:geste-stone}

The largest lever of the machine is not software (\cref{sec:bilan}). An unencrypted \texttt{D:} volume, created by a script already written that Stone would run himself as administrator (\fichier{outils/creer-disque-modeles.ps1}), would raise the ceiling of A from 6.24 to \textbf{14.1 tokens/s} in the fitted-LRU scenario if the disk reached its rated 6.0\,GB/s there \calc{} (\fichier{fondations/journaux/07-synthese/csv/architectures-gptoss120b.csv}, row \texttt{A\_sans\_perte\_disque\_non\_chiffre\_6}). This is an upper bound: Intel RST VMD stays on the path, and its share of the 53\,\% lost is not separated. Beyond that, the link takes over (58\,ms against 67 for the disk): keeping the link at Gen 4 and VRAM capacity become levers again.
